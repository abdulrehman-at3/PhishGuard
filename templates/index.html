How the Engine Reads a Message

Four checks run on every submission, in this order.

I

Message Classification

Identifies what the message claims to be — Marketing, a Security / Account Notice, a Transactional receipt, or a Newsletter — before judging it.

II

Sender Verification

Compares the sending address against known brand domains and free-mail providers to catch impersonation.

III

Link & Credential Analysis

Checks every URL for lookalike domains and insecure protocols, and distinguishes a code being delivered to you from a genuine request to send one back.

IV

Risk Scoring

Weighs hard evidence far more heavily than wording alone, so ordinary marketing or verification email isn't penalized for sounding urgent.

Quick Safety Tips

Hover over links before clicking to preview the real destination.
Never share passwords or one-time passcodes over email.
Enable multi-factor authentication wherever it's offered.
Type known websites directly into your browser instead of clicking links.
When in doubt, contact the organization using a number you already trust.
Report suspicious emails to your IT or security team.
